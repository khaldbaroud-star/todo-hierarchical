Privacy Policy

تطبيق المهام الهرمي (Hierarchical Tasks)

Last updated: November 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how تطبيق المهام الهرمي ("the App", "we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our hierarchical task management application.

1. Information We Collect

The information processed by the App depends on how you use it.

  • Tasks and lists: Task titles, descriptions where provided, due dates, completion status, list names, hierarchical relationships, and related timestamps.
  • Device identifier: A randomly generated identifier used to associate data with a device when you use the App without signing in.
  • Google account information: If you use Google Sign-In, the App processes your Google account's unique identifier and email address to establish your account identity. Google may also provide basic profile information, such as your name and profile picture, through its authentication service. The current backend does not store the name or profile picture.
  • Authentication information: When Google Sign-In is used, the App creates a signed session token containing an account identifier, email address, and session timestamps. The intended session duration is 90 days.

2. Local Storage and Synchronization

The App uses IndexedDB in your browser or device, through the Dexie library, to store tasks and lists locally. This allows you to use the App without creating an account or maintaining an internet connection.

If synchronization is used, task and list information is transmitted to our backend and stored in a Cloudflare D1 database. This allows your information to be retrieved and synchronized across devices using the same account identity or device identifier.

Google Sign-In is designed to be optional. Users can use the App without signing in. When signed in, the account identity is used to associate synchronized information with the Google account.

3. How We Use Information

We use processed information only as necessary to:

  • Provide task and list management features.
  • Save and retrieve your tasks and lists.
  • Synchronize information between your devices.
  • Authenticate users who choose Google Sign-In.
  • Maintain the security and operation of the App.
  • Respond to privacy and support requests.

4. Google Sign-In

If you choose to sign in with Google, authentication is provided through Google Identity Services. Google provides an identity token that our backend verifies using Google's public verification keys.

We use the Google account's unique identifier and email address to establish your account identity and create an application session. We do not use Google Sign-In to access your Google Drive, Gmail, contacts, or other Google services.

Your use of Google authentication is also subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Third-Party Services

The App relies on the following services for its operation:

  • Google: Provides account authentication when you choose to sign in with Google.
  • Cloudflare: Provides the infrastructure used to host the application backend and store synchronized information in Cloudflare D1.
  • Google Fonts: The App may load fonts from Google Fonts to display its interface.

These providers may process information as necessary to provide their respective services, subject to their own privacy policies and terms.

We do not sell your personal information or use task information for advertising.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored information remains in your browser or device until you delete it or clear the relevant browser storage. Clearing local storage may remove information that has not been synchronized.

Synchronized information may remain in our Cloudflare D1 database until it is deleted. Records marked as deleted may be retained as synchronization records rather than being immediately physically erased.

You may request deletion of account-associated information by contacting us at the email address below. Please include the Google account email associated with your request. We will review and process deletion requests subject to applicable legal obligations and technical limitations.

Signing out does not, by itself, delete locally stored tasks or synchronized information.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect information, including HTTPS connections, server-side verification of Google identity tokens, and signed application session tokens.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of information.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not specifically directed at children, and we do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable privacy laws. If you believe a child has provided personal information that should be deleted, please contact us.

9. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information.

You can choose whether to use Google Sign-In. You can also contact us with questions or requests concerning your data.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's features, data practices, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
